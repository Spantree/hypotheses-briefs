# Pricing Hypotheses

## Pricing Philosophy

Our pricing strategy reflects Spantree's value-based approach while acknowledging the capital-intensive nature of drone deployments. We position ourselves as the premium full-service provider, justifying higher prices through superior outcomes, comprehensive services, and our unique collaborative approach.

## Pricing Model Architecture

### Three-Tier Value Framework

**Tier 1: Technology Foundation**
- Hardware costs (drone, dock, infrastructure): ~$75K-$150K
- Represents 20-30% of total project value
- Commodity pricing pressure expected
- Margin opportunity through volume purchasing

**Tier 2: Professional Services Premium**
- Implementation and integration: $150K-$500K
- Represents 50-60% of initial project value
- High margin opportunity (65-75%)
- Differentiated by Spantree expertise

**Tier 3: Ongoing Value Creation**
- Subscription and managed services: $120K-$600K/year
- Represents 70% of lifetime customer value
- Recurring revenue with 80%+ margins
- Scales with customer success

## Pricing Psychology and Positioning

### Value Anchoring Strategy

1. **Start with Problem Cost**
   - "Your current inventory inaccuracy costs $1.2M annually"
   - "Safety incidents average $225K per year"
   - "Manual counting disrupts operations for 90 days"

2. **Present Transformation Value**
   - "Achieve 99.5% accuracy within 30 days"
   - "Eliminate safety risks completely"
   - "Real-time visibility transforms operations"

3. **Price as Investment**
   - "Investment of $580K delivers $1.2M annual value"
   - "6-month payback with 200% ROI"
   - "Includes everything needed for success"
