### Enterprise Considerations
This tier acknowledges Fortune 500 procurement realities:
- 6-12 month sales cycles with multiple stakeholder reviews
- Complex governance and security requirements
- Higher margins to offset extended engagement costs
- Documentation and compliance overhead
- Strategic value justifying bureaucratic investment
