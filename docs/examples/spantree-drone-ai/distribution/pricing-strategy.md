# Pricing Strategy

## Philosophy: Value-Based with Strategic Flexibility

Our pricing strategy reflects Spantree/Trifork's commitment to delivering exceptional value while building sustainable, profitable relationships. In the enterprise drone services market, we position ourselves as the premium full-service provider, justifying higher prices through superior outcomes, comprehensive services, and our unique collaborative approach that transforms customers into self-sufficient operators rather than dependent clients.

The philosophy acknowledges that in enterprise technology services, the lowest price rarely wins. Instead, sophisticated buyers evaluate total cost of ownership, risk mitigation, and strategic value creation. Our pricing must communicate confidence in our solution's ability to deliver promised outcomes while providing flexibility to address diverse customer situations.

## Hybrid Product and Services Model

Our pricing strategy reflects a sophisticated hybrid approach that productizes core capabilities while maintaining professional services for customer-specific value creation. This model addresses the market reality that no two enterprise deployments are identical, yet common patterns enable standardization of foundational components.

### Productized Components (40-50% of value)
- Base technology stack including drone control, image capture, and processing pipelines
- Standard AI models for common asset types (vehicles, containers, equipment)
- Core visualization dashboards and reporting tools
- Integration frameworks for major enterprise systems

### Professional Services Layers (50-60% of value)
- Custom AI model training for specialized assets
- Business process integration adapting to existing workflows
- Site-specific optimization for unique layouts and constraints
- Industry-specific analytics addressing vertical requirements
- Change management programs ensuring successful adoption

This hybrid model creates multiple advantages: faster time-to-value through pre-built components, lower total costs compared to fully custom development, higher quality through proven components, flexibility for unique requirements, and a scalable growth model.

## Three-Tier Value Creation Architecture

### Foundation Layer: Technology Infrastructure
Hardware and software components representing 20-30% of total project value:
- Drones, docking stations, and computing infrastructure: $75,000-$150,000 per facility
- Strategic partnerships like General Pacific (Genpac) for hardware procurement
- Volume purchasing agreements enabling competitive pricing
- Focus on transparency rather than maximum margins

### Differentiation Layer: Professional Services
Core value creation representing 50-60% of initial project value:
- Implementation and integration: $150,000-$500,000
- 65-75% gross margins fueling growth and innovation
- System design and architecture services
- Custom AI model development
- Integration engineering
- Change management and training programs

### Transformation Layer: Ongoing Value
Recurring revenue representing 70% of customer lifetime value:
- Subscriptions: $120,000-$600,000 annually
- 80%+ gross margins
- Software updates and new features
- Managed services options
- Advanced analytics and reporting
- Continuous model improvement
