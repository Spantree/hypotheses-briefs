Our primary distribution channel leverages Spantree/Trifork's proven enterprise sales approach, enhanced with drone-specific expertise that addresses the unique aspects of autonomous systems deployment. The enterprise drone market demands a sophisticated sales approach that combines technical credibility with business acumen, regulatory knowledge with operational understanding, and strategic vision with practical implementation experience.

## Building the Sales Team

Building a specialized sales team requires careful orchestration of complementary skills:

- **Technical Sales Engineers** with drone and AI expertise provide the credibility necessary to engage with innovation teams and technical evaluators
- **Industry Veterans** from logistics and manufacturing bring the operational context that resonates with business stakeholders who care more about outcomes than technology
- **Regulatory Compliance Specialists** navigate the complex web of federal, state, and local requirements that can make or break deployments
- **Customer Success Managers** ensure long-term relationships that transform initial projects into strategic partnerships, recognizing that in enterprise sales, the real work begins after the contract is signed

## Sales Process Architecture

The enterprise sales journey for drone solutions differs markedly from traditional software sales, requiring a carefully orchestrated process that builds confidence while addressing legitimate concerns about new technology adoption.

### The Awareness Stage (Months 1-2)
Focuses on education and credibility building. Thought leadership content positions Spantree/Trifork as a trusted advisor rather than just another vendor. Executive briefings translate drone capabilities into business outcomes that resonate with C-suite priorities. Webinars featuring customer success stories provide social proof that drone solutions deliver real value. SEO-optimized content ensures we're discoverable when enterprises begin their research journey. The key during this stage is establishing ourselves as the safe choice for risk-averse enterprise buyers.

### The Consideration Stage (Months 2-4)
Transforms interest into serious evaluation. Site assessments reveal specific opportunities while demonstrating our operational expertise. ROI calculators quantify potential value using the customer's own data and assumptions. Stakeholder alignment workshops address the political realities of enterprise technology adoption, ensuring all affected parties understand benefits and have their concerns addressed. Regulatory compliance roadmaps remove a major source of anxiety by clearly outlining the path to legal operations. This stage builds the business case that will ultimately drive purchase decisions.

### The Decision Stage (Months 4-6)
Converts evaluation into commitment through proof and negotiation. Proof of concept deployments at customer facilities demonstrate that solutions work in their specific environment, not just in controlled demos. Integration architecture reviews ensure compatibility with existing systems, addressing IT concerns about security, data flow, and maintenance. Safety and compliance documentation satisfies legal and risk management requirements. Executive presentations synthesize technical and business value for final decision makers. Contract negotiations balance customer risk mitigation needs with our business model requirements.

### The Implementation Stage
Transforms promises into reality through systematic deployment. Implementation timelines vary significantly based on project scope and complexity:

- **Small deployments** (single facility, standard integrations): 3-4 months
- **Typical enterprise deployments** (custom AI, multiple integrations): 6-12 months  
- **Complex multi-site rollouts**: Up to 18 months

This extended timeline reflects the realities of enterprise deployment:
- Regulatory approvals and waiver processes
- Infrastructure preparation and installation
- Custom AI model development and training
- Enterprise system integration and testing
- Comprehensive staff training across shifts
- Phased rollout with careful monitoring

Rather than rushing to premature go-live, our approach emphasizes thorough preparation that ensures sustainable success. Clients appreciate this realistic timeline that sets proper expectations and reduces implementation risk.
