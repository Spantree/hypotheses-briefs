#### Regulatory Analysis

**Illinois** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

- [725 ILCS 167/ - Freedom from Drone Surveillance Act](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=3526): Law enforcement restrictions only
- **Commercial Operations**: Follow FAA Part 107, no additional state restrictions
- **Strategic Advantage**: Industrial-friendly regulations

**Texas** ⭐⭐⭐

- [Texas Government Code Chapter 423](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/GV/htm/GV.423.htm): Comprehensive restrictions
- **Critical Infrastructure**: Class B misdemeanor for flying below 400ft over facilities
- **Challenge**: Multiple criminal penalties but largest industrial base

**California** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

- [Civil Code Section 1708.8](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1708.8): Privacy protection focus
- **State Parks**: Generally require permits for commercial operations
- **Opportunity**: Massive industrial presence despite privacy requirements

#### FAA Data & BVLOS Progress

- **Current Status**: Only [56 total BVLOS waivers as of June 2020](https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators/part_107_waivers/)
- **Biden Administration**: [Executive Order "Unleashing American Drone Dominance"](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/06/) mandates BVLOS rulemaking within 30 days
- **Canada Advantage**: [Full BVLOS implementation by November 4, 2025](https://tc.canada.ca/en/aviation/drone-safety/) for drones up to 150kg
