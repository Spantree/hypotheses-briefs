# Deep Research Prompt: Regulatory Landscape for Commercial Drone Operations

## Research Objective
Provide a comprehensive analysis of the regulatory environment for commercial drone operations, focusing on requirements for autonomous operations, beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) flights, and data privacy considerations.

## Key Research Areas

### 1. United States Regulations
- **FAA Part 107**: Current rules and limitations
- **BVLOS Waivers**: Application process, approval rates, requirements
- **Remote ID Requirements**: Implementation timeline and compliance
- **Drone-in-a-Box Regulations**: Specific considerations for autonomous operations
- **State and Local Laws**: Variations and additional requirements
- **Proposed Rule Changes**: Future regulatory roadmap

### 2. International Regulations
- **European Union (EASA)**: Specific category operations
- **Canada (Transport Canada)**: RPAS regulations
- **Australia (CASA)**: ReOC and RePL requirements
- **UK (CAA)**: Post-Brexit regulatory framework
- **Key differences**: Operational limitations by country
