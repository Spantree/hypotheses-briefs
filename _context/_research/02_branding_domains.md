# Deep Research Prompt: Branding and Domain Strategy for Drone Vision AI Business

## Research Objective
Develop branding recommendations and identify available domain names for a professional services company specializing in full-stack drone solutions with vision AI capabilities.

## Key Research Areas

### 1. Industry Naming Conventions
- Analyze naming patterns in the drone industry
- Identify trends in AI/ML company naming
- Review professional services firm naming strategies
- Look for naming conventions that convey trust, expertise, and innovation

### 2. Domain Availability Research
Search for available .com, .ai, .io domains that combine these concepts:
- Drone/UAV/Aerial + Vision/Sight/View
- Sky/Air/Flight + Intelligence/AI/Analytics
- Inventory/Asset/Track + Drone/Aerial
- Professional terms: Solutions, Systems, Intelligence, Analytics, Insights

### 3. Brand Name Suggestions
Generate names that:
- Convey expertise in both drones and AI
- Sound professional and enterprise-focused
- Are easy to pronounce and remember
- Work internationally
- Avoid trademark conflicts

### 4. Competitive Brand Analysis
- How are competitors naming their services?
- What messaging do they use?
- What visual identity trends exist in the space?
- What brand archetypes are common?

### 5. SEO and Digital Presence
- Keyword analysis for drone vision AI services
- Search volume for relevant terms
- Competition levels for key phrases
- Social media handle availability

### 6. Trademark Considerations
- Check USPTO for similar marks in relevant classes
- International trademark databases
- Common law trademark usage
- Domain history and reputation

## Specific Areas to Explore

### Potential Name Categories
1. **Descriptive**: DroneVisionPro, AerialAIServices
2. **Abstract**: Skyward, Vantage, Elevate
3. **Compound**: SightFlight, ViewPoint Aerial
4. **Technical**: Neural Sky, Cognitive Drone Systems
5. **Professional**: [Company] Aerial Intelligence, [Company] Drone Solutions

### Domain Extensions Priority
1. .com (highest priority)
2. .ai (AI-focused branding)
3. .io (tech-focused)
4. .solutions (descriptive)
5. .systems (enterprise-focused)

## Output Requirements
1. Top 20 brand name suggestions with available domains
2. Pros/cons analysis for each suggestion
3. SEO potential assessment
4. Trademark risk assessment
5. Brand positioning statement examples
6. Tagline suggestions that work with the brand names
