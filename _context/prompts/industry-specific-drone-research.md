# Industry-Specific Deep Research Prompts for Drone AI Applications

## Business Context

**Company**: Trifork Drone AI Solutions (branded as Acumulus by Trifork)

**Industry Focus**: We're targeting specific verticals where large-scale outdoor inventory management creates significant operational challenges:
- **Rail and Intermodal**: Managing railcars across massive yards with complex switching operations
- **Automotive**: Tracking thousands of vehicles through manufacturing and logistics stages
- **Ports**: Container visibility and stack optimization across hundreds of acres
- **Energy/Utilities**: Infrastructure asset management and spare parts inventory
- **Construction/Mining**: Material stockpile measurement and equipment tracking

**Value Proposition**: Our domain-specific solutions combine drone hardware, custom AI models trained on industry assets, and deep integration with sector-specific systems (like Railinc for rail or TOS for ports).

**Key Industry Hypotheses**:
- Each vertical has unique pain points that generic drone solutions can't address
- Industry-specific AI models and integrations justify premium pricing
- Domain expertise creates competitive moats and accelerates sales cycles
- Vertical references are critical for enterprise credibility

## Rail and Intermodal Logistics Research

**Research Objective**: Understand the specific challenges and opportunities for drone deployment in rail yards and intermodal facilities.

**Key Questions**:
- How many Class I rail yards exceed 50 acres in North America?
- What are current dwell time fees and how much do they cost the industry?
- What percentage of rail cars are "lost" or misplaced at any given time?
- How do railroads currently track car locations and what are the gaps?
- What are the safety statistics specific to rail yard operations?
- Which railroads have tested or deployed drone technology?

**Specific Data Points Needed**:
- Average rail yard sizes and car capacities
- Typical inventory accuracy rates in rail
- Cost of manual car counting and inspection
- Regulatory requirements for rail car tracking
- Integration requirements with Railinc and railroad APIs

## Research Methodology Notes

1. **Primary Sources**: Industry associations, government databases, regulatory filings
2. **Secondary Sources**: Analyst reports, case studies, conference proceedings
3. **Validation Methods**: Cross-reference multiple sources, seek contrarian views
4. **Update Frequency**: Quarterly for fast-changing metrics, annually for structural data
5. **Geographic Focus**: North America primary, but note global best practices
