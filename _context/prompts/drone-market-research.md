# Deep Research Prompts for Drone AI Market Intelligence

## Business Context

**Company**: Trifork Drone AI Solutions (branded as Acumulus by Trifork)

**Value Proposition**: We provide full-stack enterprise inventory intelligence through comprehensive professional services that combine autonomous drone operations, advanced AI/computer vision, and deep enterprise integration expertise. Unlike platform-only providers or hardware vendors, we deliver end-to-end transformation of how enterprises manage physical assets across large outdoor facilities.

**Target Market**: Large enterprises with 50+ acre facilities in logistics, manufacturing, ports, rail yards, and energy sectors who struggle with inventory accuracy, safety risks, and operational visibility.

**Key Hypotheses to Validate**:
- Enterprises need comprehensive professional services, not just drone technology
- The market is ready for a "resegmented" approach combining full-stack capabilities
- Our $500K-$800K professional package pricing will resonate with enterprise buyers
- Safety, accuracy, and time savings justify 12-month ROI requirements

## Research Instructions

Please provide all research findings in **markdown format** with **inline citations** using markdown links. For example:
- According to [DroneDeploy's 2024 State of Drone Industry Report](https://www.dronedeploy.com/resources/), construction firms report 80% time savings...
- The [FAA's Part 107 database](https://www.faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators/) shows 350,000+ certified pilots as of 2024...

Structure responses with clear headers, bullet points, and data tables where appropriate. Always link to primary sources when available.

## Execution Notes

For each research prompt:
1. Start with free/public sources before considering paid reports
2. Document all sources with dates for future updates
3. Look for contrarian views that challenge our assumptions
4. Prioritize recent (2023-2025) data over older sources
5. Cross-reference multiple sources to validate key claims
6. Note regional variations that might affect go-to-market strategy
