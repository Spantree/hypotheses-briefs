# Customer Validation and Discovery Research Prompts

## Business Context

**Company**: Trifork Drone AI Solutions (branded as Acumulus by Trifork)

**Customer Archetypes We're Validating**:
1. **Sarah Chen** - Operations Director at large logistics facility managing 750K sq ft, $65M budget, 350 employees
2. **Marcus Rodriguez** - Innovation VP at automotive manufacturer, tasked with digital transformation across 12 plants
3. **David Thompson** - Facilities Innovation Manager in energy sector, balancing safety and efficiency
4. **Lisa Washington** - Port Operations Executive managing 500-acre container terminal

**Core Customer Hypotheses**:
- Enterprise buyers prioritize comprehensive solutions over point technologies
- Safety improvements are as important as ROI in decision-making
- 12-month payback requirements are standard for technology investments
- Integration with existing systems (WMS, ERP) is a critical requirement
- Decision committees include Operations, IT, Finance, Safety, and Legal stakeholders

**Detractor Personalities** (from "Driving Technical Change"):
- The Uninformed, The Herd, The Cynic, The Burned, The Time Crunched, The Boss

## Research Instructions

Please provide findings in **markdown format** with **inline citations**. Structure your response as:

1. **Executive Summary** - Key insights that validate or challenge our hypotheses
2. **Detailed Findings** - Organized by customer archetype and topic
3. **Evidence Table** - Quantitative data supporting conclusions
4. **Contradictory Evidence** - Findings that challenge our assumptions
5. **Recommendations** - Suggested pivots based on research

Example citations:
- A [McKinsey survey of 800 supply chain executives](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/) found that 73% cite inventory accuracy as top-3 priority...
- [LinkedIn analysis of 50 Operations Directors](https://www.linkedin.com/search/) shows average tenure of 3.2 years in role...

- Identify common connections who might provide introductions

## Validation Methodology

1. **Hypothesis Documentation**: Clearly state each assumption to be validated
2. **Evidence Collection**: Gather data from multiple sources
3. **Contradiction Seeking**: Actively look for disconfirming evidence
4. **Pattern Recognition**: Identify themes across data points
5. **Pivot Readiness**: Be prepared to adjust strategy based on findings
6. **Continuous Validation**: Plan for ongoing customer discovery post-launch
